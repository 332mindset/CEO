The people behind the armor.

Iron Man Mark 85: modeled by HarlowFX and published on Sketchfab by LLIypuk.

The model is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. For this website, the geometry was simplified and compressed with Draco, textures were converted to WebP, and materials and lighting were adjusted for the browser. Shoulder, elbow, wrist, neck and torso joints were added, with rigid armor plates attached for animation. An inner mechanical frame closes the exposed joints.

Iron Man is a Marvel character. This is an independent fan-made portfolio; the model creators and Marvel are not affiliated with this website.
